Most small teams juggle a spreadsheet for documents, another for policies, a third for printed stock and a to-do app on the side. Workspace puts them together, so a brochure links to its print order, and a policy review becomes a task.

Dashboard

Know what needs you, the moment you open it.

The dashboard pulls the urgent things out of every register: tasks due today, documents and policies past their review date, items running low and print orders that are late.

  • Live counts for tasks, reviews, stock on hand and stock on order
  • Overdue items flagged in red, with what's coming up next
  • Quick actions: new document, new task, issue stock for an event
  • Light, dark or automatic appearance
Planner

A planner that knows about your documents.

Organise work into buckets, then see it the way that suits the moment. Tasks can link to a document, an event or a policy, and you can track who a draft is with for review or approval.

  • My day, grid, board, calendar and charts views
  • Group by bucket, progress, priority, due date, label or person
  • Subtasks, labels, priorities, comments, links and repeating tasks
  • Send for review or approval, with a ready-made email draft
  • Drag and drop between columns
Document register

Every controlled document, and which version is current.

Keep one source of truth for your forms, templates, factsheets, guidelines and design files. Workspace records who owns each one, when it changed and when it's next due for review.

  • Reference numbers, categories, classification and status
  • Major and minor versions, with the previous version kept alongside
  • Check out and check in, so two people don't edit at once
  • Review cycles that roll the next review date forward
  • Full change history, table or board view, CSV and Excel export
Policy register

Build out your policy framework, gap by gap.

Map every policy, procedure, form and plan across governance, finance, services, people and risk. Track each one from "to be developed" through to approved, and keep reviews on schedule.

  • Eight stages, from to be developed to retired, on a drag-and-drop pipeline
  • Risk ratings, approval levels, owners and review cycles by document type
  • Review schedule and a tracker for who each document is with
  • Insights on how complete the framework is and where the risk sits
  • Data checks that spot gaps and inconsistencies
  • Import from your existing spreadsheet, export to Excel
Marketing stock

Never run out of brochures the week of a big event.

Track printed material and branded merchandise from the printer to the event table. Workspace works out how fast each item is used and tells you when to reprint, allowing for the printer's lead time.

  • Inventory with reorder levels, locations, suppliers and unit costs
  • Reorder planner with suggested quantities and estimated cost
  • Print and merchandise orders, from quote to proof to delivery
  • Issue stock to an event, with who collected it and for which team
  • Event costs using the landed cost of each order
  • Stock takes that record any difference as an adjustment

Questions

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage, on the device you're using. It isn't sent to any server. That also means each browser keeps its own copy, so use backup and restore to move between computers.

Can my whole team use it together?

Out of the box, Workspace is single-user: each person's browser has its own data. To share a register, one person can keep it and send exported files or backups to others. Real-time sharing would need a small back end, and the storage layer is written so one can be added.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your Workspace data is cleared too. Download a full backup regularly so you can restore it.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens, and the sidebar folds into a menu.

Can I change the categories, departments and stages?

Yes. They're defined in short lists near the top of the script in app/index.html. Edit them in any text editor and reload.

Is it really free?

Yes. Workspace is open source under the MIT licence. You can host your own copy for free on GitHub Pages, Netlify or any static host.
